What's New in Version 2.0

New

  • YouTube subscriptions: follow channels and save playlists without a Google account. Press Ctrl+U for the newest videos of your channels, Ctrl+Shift+U to manage them, and Ctrl+S in the results window to subscribe to a video's channel.
  • Minutes to load ahead: a YouTube setting that limits how much of a video is loaded in advance.
  • Favorites and subscriptions backup: export and import both lists from Preferences, Backup and restore.
  • Switch to the player from File Explorer: a General option that brings the player forward when you open a file while it runs.

Improved

  • YouTube search now uses the search language and region you set.
  • A better Arabic translation, and a new user guide in English and Arabic. Press F1 to open it.
  • The installer speaks Arabic, English and Czech, and no longer makes itself the default program for your file types. Choose it yourself in Windows Default apps if you want.

Fixed

  • Reset settings now updates all Preferences pages at once.
  • Home, End, Delete and Backspace now work in global shortcuts.
  • A video file opened together with its folder now plays first.
  • Seek step names and the YouTube components message now appear in your language.
  • YouTube results no longer jump to the next video on their own, and search no longer fails when you open it again.
